03 Manuscript under review — Transactions in Medical Imaging

TranStain: Vision Transformer based histopathology image normalization (Center for Machine Vision and Signal Processing (CMVS) - ITEE, Oulu University)

A stain normalization method using a Residual Swin Transformer generator within a CycleGAN framework — one of the earliest Swin Transformer-based CycleGAN generators applied to histopathology. Requires no paired images or expert-selected reference, and generalizes well across four datasets with unseen staining conditions.
