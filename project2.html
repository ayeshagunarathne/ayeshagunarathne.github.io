Knee bone segmentation in MRIs and Automatic Osteophyte Severity classification (HST unit, Faculty of Medicine, Oulu University)

Problem

This was the first research work to automatically classify the severity of osteophytes in the patellar bone.

Approach

Tested several methods — including local binary patterns-based, spherical harmonics-based, and machine learning-based methods — to classify osteophytes based on shape and texture variations using MRIs.
