CASE / 01
PyTorch Medical Imaging Segmentation

Problem

This work was completed at the Health Science and Technology Unit, Faculty of Medicine, University of Oulu, Finland. The main goal was to segment the patellar bone (kneecap) from a well-established public dataset, the Osteoarthritis Initiative (OAI). Very few prior attempts had been made to automatically segment the patellar bone from the OAI dataset (DESS sequence), which was identified as the research gap addressed by this work. The project used the MONAI framework for segmentation and also released an annotated dataset of the patellar bone in the OAI dataset (DESS sequence).
